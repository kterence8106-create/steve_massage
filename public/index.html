Support & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing for your session, digital payments, and parlor policies.

What should I wear or prepare for my massage appointment?
Wear comfortable clothing. You will be provided with a private dressing room, plush bathrobes, and disposable undergarments. During therapy, professional draping techniques ensure you are always modestly covered except for the specific body area being massaged.
How does M-Pesa digital payment work?
During online checkout, choose Safaricom M-Pesa. Enter your Safaricom mobile phone number, and you will receive an automatic STK PIN prompt on your phone for immediate confirmation. You also receive an instant SMS receipt with your unique reference number.
Can I reschedule or cancel my booking?
Yes, you can easily reschedule or cancel up to 2 hours prior to your scheduled time directly from your Client Portal or by contacting our front desk at +254 712 345 678.
Are therapist consultations private and confidential?
Absolutely. In accordance with the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019), all health notes, pressure preferences, and personal details are encrypted and kept strictly between you and your licensed therapist.
